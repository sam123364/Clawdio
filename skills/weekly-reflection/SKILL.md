# Weekly Reflection

## Procedure
1. Aggregate `MEMORY.md` stats for the last 7 days.
2. Calculate:
   - Total Intentional Wins (Specific)
   - Total Habit Slip-ups (Vague)
   - Most distracting time of day
3. Provide a "Digital Discipline Score" (1-100).
